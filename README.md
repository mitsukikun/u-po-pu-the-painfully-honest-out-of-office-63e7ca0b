# The Painfully Honest Out-of-Office

A single-sentence out-of-office auto-reply that says the quiet part out loud — kindly. Instead of the usual fiction that every email will be answered promptly, it admits the private truth everyone already knows (nothing gets read until the sender is back) and turns that honesty into a promise: a reply with a clear head, not a rushed one. Warm, professional, and safe to forward to a manager.

> I'm on leave and finally admitting what we all know: I won't read this until I'm back, and I'll reply then with a clear head and a proper answer instead of a rushed one.
